Current Research

My current research investigates the three-dimensional organisation of chromatin and its role in regulating the dynamics of transcription factors and DNA repair proteins. I use coarse-grained polymer models, Monte Carlo simulations, and theoretical analysis to understand nuclear architecture and its functional consequences.
